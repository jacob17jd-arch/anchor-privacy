# Privacy Policy

**App:** Anchor: Bible Reader & Audio (package: com.dijojames.anchorbible)
**Developer:** Dijo James
**Effective date:** 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the Anchor: Bible Reader & Audio app ("the app", "Anchor") handles information. We keep it simple: the app does not require an account, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to register, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. We do not run our own servers that store information about you.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The app saves some data only on your own device so it can work properly: your chosen translation, theme (dark or light), reading position, font and audio settings, and any Bible chapters you download for offline use. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

## 3. Advertising

The app may show advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks (for example, Google AdMob). These providers may collect and use certain device information, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device details, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This happens under the privacy policies of those providers, not ours. For Google's practices, see https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

## 4. Text-to-speech

Audio reading uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. We do not receive or store the text or audio you listen to.

## 5. Downloads and internet use

The app connects to the internet to load Bible text and to download chapters for offline reading. These requests are made over secure connections where available. We do not link these requests to your identity.

## 6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only sharing that occurs is the device-level data sent to advertising providers described in section 3.

## 7. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove locally stored data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and update the effective date above.
