# Privacy Policy

**App:** Anchor Audio Bible: Offline (Audio Bible)
**Developer:** Dijo James
**Effective date:** 19 November 2026

This policy explains how the Anchor Audio Bible app ("the app", "Anchor") handles information. The app does not require an account, does not show advertising, and we do not collect personal information ourselves.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to register, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details. We do not run our own servers that store information about you.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The app saves some data only on your own device so it can work properly: your chosen translation, theme (dark or light), reading position, font, audio and background-chant settings, and any Bible chapters you download for offline use. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

## 3. Advertising

The app does not contain advertising and does not use your device's advertising ID.

## 4. Scripture companion (AI feature)

The app includes an optional scripture companion powered by Google's Gemini AI service. If you choose to use it, the text you type to the companion, together with the related Bible passage, is sent over a secure connection to Google's Gemini service to generate a reply. We do not store this text on our own servers or link it to your identity. Google processes it under its own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Please do not enter personal or sensitive information into the companion. If you do not use the companion, no such data is sent.

## 5. Text-to-speech

Audio reading uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. We do not receive or store the text or audio you listen to.

## 6. Downloads and internet use

The app connects to the internet to load Bible text, to download chapters for offline reading and, if you use it, to reach the scripture companion. These requests are made over secure connections where available. We do not link these requests to your identity.

## 7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only information that leaves your device is the companion text described in section 4, which is sent to Google to provide that feature.

## 8. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal information on our side, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove locally stored data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the updated version at this address and update the effective date above.
